## Requirements
### Requirement: Gludio and Dion as C and D Grade Transition Markets
The system SHALL maintain 15 fixed FakeShops in Gludio and 15 fixed FakeShops in Dion as transition markets, offering D-Grade and C-Grade equipment, weapons, armors, jewelry, shots, enchants, and consumables.

#### Scenario: Player visits Gludio or Dion market
- **WHEN** a real player enters Gludio or Dion
- **THEN** the system SHALL display 15 FakeShops offering D-Grade and C-Grade transition gear and supplies

### Requirement: Giran as C to S Grade Mega Trading Hub
The system SHALL establish Giran Town as a mega trading hub hosting 50 fixed FakeShops covering C-Grade, B-Grade, A-Grade, and S-Grade items, including enchanted weapon/armor sets (+4 to +8), refined materials, dwarven crafting workshops, and buying stores.

#### Scenario: Player visits Giran mega market
- **WHEN** a real player enters Giran Town
- **THEN** the system SHALL display 50 active FakeShops offering C, B, A, and S Grade equipment, enchanted items, and crafting services

### Requirement: Aden as 20-Shop Elite High-Five Marketplace
The system SHALL establish Aden Castle Town as an elite marketplace hosting 20 fixed FakeShops specializing in S80 and S84 tier items, including enchanted weapons and armors (+4, +6, +8) from Dynasty, Icarus, Vesper Noble, Vorpal, and Elegy sets, Attribute Stones and Crystals (Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Dark, Holy), Scroll Enchant Weapon/Armor S (Normal & Blessed), Crystals S, Gemstones S, and Top-Grade Life Stones (Lvl 80/84).

#### Scenario: Player visits Aden elite marketplace
- **WHEN** a real player opens a shop in Aden Castle Town
- **THEN** the store SHALL offer enchanted S80/S84 weapons and armors, attribute stones/crystals, S-grade scrolls, and high-level life stones across 20 distinct shops

### Requirement: Local Supply Shops for Secondary Cities
The system SHALL configure secondary cities (Oren, Hunters Village, Heine, Goddard, Rune, Schuttgart) to host 5 fixed FakeShops each, restricted strictly to basic supplies (potions, scrolls, local shots) and primary raw materials.

#### Scenario: Player visits a secondary city
- **WHEN** a real player enters any secondary city
- **THEN** the system SHALL display exactly 5 FakeShops offering exclusively basic supplies and primary raw materials
